## Draft: Teaching predictive modeling without losing the human

- **Tags:** Data · Teaching
- **Question:** How can predictive modeling become approachable for learners who do not identify as “math people”?
- **What I tried:** Start with a concrete decision, use plain-language features and outcomes, then connect the model back to the people affected by the prediction.
- **What I learned:** Good technical education is not about removing complexity; it is about giving people a confident path through it.
- **See the work:** Draft only; public workshop materials are not linked yet.

## Draft: Testing AI tools before trusting the output

- **Tags:** AI · Automation
- **Question:** What makes an AI-assisted workflow useful rather than merely impressive?
- **What I tried:** Compare outputs against source data, define failure cases, protect sensitive information, and keep a human decision-maker in the loop.
- **What I learned:** Reliable AI work needs validation and governance designed in from the beginning.
- **See the work:** Draft only; sanitized examples are planned.

## Draft: Learning robotics by building and debugging

- **Tags:** Robotics · Teaching
- **Question:** What does robotics teach us about turning a technical idea into something people can use?
- **What I tried:** Build small, testable behaviors, observe what the system actually does, and treat debugging as part of the learning experience.
- **What I learned:** The fastest route to confidence is a small loop of build, test, explain, and try again.
- **See the work:** Draft only; workshop materials are not linked yet.
